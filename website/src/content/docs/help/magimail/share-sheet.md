Magimail ships with a native macOS Share Sheet extension, enabling you to draft emails and attach files from anywhere in the operating system without switching apps first.

## Sharing Files from Finder

To email documents, archives, or images directly from Finder:

1. Right-click any file (or select multiple files) in Finder.
2. Select **Share → Magimail**.
3. Magimail instantly opens a compose draft with your selected files attached.
4. Choose your sending account (or let Magimail use your active account), enter your recipient, and send. [Multiple Accounts](/help/magimail/accounts/) covers the account list.

## Sharing Web Links from Safari

When browsing in Safari or any other macOS browser:

1. Click the system **Share** button in the browser toolbar (or choose **File → Share**).
2. Select **Magimail**.
3. Magimail creates a new draft pre-populated with the web page title in the subject line and the URL in the email body.

## Sharing Content Out of Magimail

You can also send email content out of Magimail into other apps:

- In the [Magimail toolbar](/help/magimail/toolbar/) or the File menu, click **Share**.
- Share thread links or excerpts directly to **Apple Notes**, **Reminders**, or **Messages**.
- Select the **Print** button dropdown in the toolbar (or press <kbd>⌘P</kbd>) and choose **Export as PDF…** to save a clean, formatted PDF archive of the email conversation.
