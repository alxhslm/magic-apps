## Showing & Hiding the Sidebar

You can toggle the sidebar at any time:

- **Menu**: Choose **View → Toggle Sidebar** from the macOS menu bar.
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press <kbd>⌥⌘S</kbd>.
- **Toolbar**: Add the **Sidebar** button to your native window toolbar from **View → Customise Toolbar…** for one-click access.

When opened, Magical automatically adjusts the window's minimum width (expanding by 270pt to 1,470pt) so your primary Google Calendar grid has plenty of space. Closing the sidebar smoothly restores the window.

## Keeping in Sync with Google Calendar

The companion sidebar is not a static list—it dynamically adapts to whatever view you are using in the main Google Calendar interface:

- **Month & Schedule Views (Fantastical Style)**:
  - Displays a collapsible **Mini Month Grid** at the top with density dots showing busy days.
  - Features an **Endless Schedule Agenda** below, grouping upcoming meetings and tasks chronologically.
  - A red line marks the current time of day (`● ────────────── 14:35`).
- **Week & 4-Day Views**:
  - Automatically switches to a **Week Scrubber**, showing each day of the current week with date numbers, selection rings, and activity dots.
  - Clicking any day in the scrubber focuses the agenda on that specific date.
- **Day View**:
  - Focuses on the single active day, featuring pinned all-day events at the top and an hourly day grid matching your daily schedule.

Navigating to another date in Google Calendar immediately syncs the companion sidebar, and clicking a date in the sidebar smoothly navigates Google Calendar to that day.

## Work & Personal Calendars in Harmony

The companion sidebar unifies every Google account you have connected to Magical:

- **Avoid Double-Booking**: Even if your main window is displaying your Work account, the companion sidebar shows your Personal and Family events in the same timeline.
- **Calendar Colours**: Every meeting is tinted with the exact colour of its underlying calendar, making it easy to distinguish personal appointments from team stand-ups at a glance.
- **Bidirectional Filtering**: Click the <span class="sym sym-filter" aria-hidden="true"></span> **Filter Menu** in the sidebar header to temporarily hide individual calendars. Your filter choices synchronise instantly with the [Menu Bar Popover](/help/magical/menu-bar/).

## Quick Actions & Meeting Triage

Every event card in the sidebar is fully interactive:

- **Jump Straight to Event**: Click any event in the sidebar to reveal and focus it in Google Calendar, switching accounts automatically if it belongs to another calendar.
- **1-Click Video Calls**: Click **Join** on any meeting with a Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Webex link to jump straight into the call.
- **RSVP in Place**: Answer meeting invites with **Yes**, **Maybe**, or **No** directly from the card.
- **Google Tasks**: View and complete your [Google Tasks](/help/magical/tasks/) alongside your meetings.
- **Jump to Today**: Click the month or week heading at the top of the sidebar to jump back to the current day.
